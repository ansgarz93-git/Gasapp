# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they preserve the project's small, generic scope.

1. Open an issue describing the proposed behavior.
2. Keep APIs domain-neutral and free of private/product-specific identifiers.
3. Add or update tests for behavioral changes.
4. Do not include credentials, real operational data, proprietary catalogs, or private endpoints.
5. Keep changes focused; unrelated refactors should be separate.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution is licensed under Apache-2.0.
